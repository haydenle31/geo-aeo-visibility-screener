The first screen of results was almost entirely paid placements unrelated to
the specific query — "New Permanent Teeth In 24 Hrs" (Nuvia Dental Implants)
and "Top Cosmetic Dental Services" (Ocean Pointe Dentistry) sponsored ads,
both about implants generically rather than "best cosmetic dentist in
Honolulu" specifically. A "See less sponsored results" toggle was needed to
get past them. A sign-in prompt ("Welcome back — Sign in to earn Rewards")
also covered part of the results on load.

Net finding: for this query shape, Bing's organic answer is buried under ad
inventory that isn't even well-targeted to the query — a business relying on
Bing/Copilot visibility here is competing with ad spend, not just other
dentists' content.
